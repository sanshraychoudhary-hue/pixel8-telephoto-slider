# 📋 Bill of Materials (BOM) — Pixel 8 Telephoto Slider

This document details all required 3D printed components, optical lenses, fasteners, and consumables needed to build the **Pixel 8 Visor-Glide Telephoto Slider**.

---

## 1. 3D Printed Parts

| Part Name | Qty | File | Recommended Material | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visor Rail Mount** | 1 | `cad/stl/pixel8_visor_rail.stl` | Tough Resin / PETG | Primary snap-on chassis that clamps around the visor perimeter |
| **Lens Slider Carriage** | 1 | `cad/stl/pixel8_lens_carriage.stl` | Tough Resin / PETG | Linear sliding block with Ø 15.6 mm cylindrical lens housing |

## 2. Optical Glass Element

| Component | Qty | Specifications | Recommended Donor / Source | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **2x Telephoto Optical Core** | 1 | • Outer Diameter: 14.5 mm – 15.6 mm<br>• Magnification: 2.0x to 2.5x<br>• Multi-layer broadband Anti-Reflective (AR) coating | • Apexel 85mm Portrait auxiliary lens (unscrew outer aluminum shell)<br>• Moment 58mm Tele core (v2)<br>• Sandmarc 60mm Telephoto optical block | Multi-element glass (usually 4–5 elements in 3–4 groups) yields the sharpest edge-to-edge resolution on 50MP sensors. |

---

## 3. Fasteners & Detents (Position Locking)

| Component | Qty | Specifications | Purpose |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Miniature N52 Neodymium Disc Magnets** | 4 | • Diameter: Ø 2.0 mm (or Ø 1.5 mm)<br>• Thickness: 1.0 mm<br>• Grade: N50 or N52 nickel-plated | 2 magnets embedded into the carriage + 2 embedded into the rail at the 0 mm (Parked) and 28 mm (Active) positions for positive tactile locking. |

*Note: Alternatively, small 3D-printed flexure spring tabs can be printed directly if you prefer a 100% printed solution.*

---

## 4. Protection & Consumables

| Item | Qty | Description | Purpose |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Self-Adhesive Microfiber / Velvet Tape** | 1 roll / strip | 0.3 mm thickness adhesive-backed flocked tape | Applied to the underside contact pads of the carriage to guarantee zero scratching on the phone's visor glass. |
| **Dry PTFE Lubricant / Silicone Spray** | A few drops | High-performance dry lubricant | Applied to the rail C-channels for smooth, low-friction gliding that will not attract pocket dust. |
| **B-7000 / Thin Acrylic Adhesive** | Small tube / strip | Non-permanent multi-purpose electronics adhesive | To seat the magnets securely in their recessed pockets and lightly anchor the rail mount to the phone edges if not using snap-fit tension alone. |
