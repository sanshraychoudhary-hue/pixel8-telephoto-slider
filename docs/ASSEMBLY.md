# 🔧 Assembly & Installation Guide — Pixel 8 Telephoto Slider

Step-by-step instructions for assembling the hardware, installing the optical element, and mounting the slider onto your Google Pixel 8.

---

## Required Tools & Materials
* 3D printed **Visor Rail Mount** and **Lens Slider Carriage**
* 2x Telephoto optical core (see [BOM.md](BOM.md))
* 4x N52 miniature disc magnets (Ø 2mm × 1mm)
* 0.3mm adhesive-backed microfiber or velvet tape
* B-7000 electronics glue or superglue (cyanoacrylate)
* Wooden toothpick (for precise glue application)
* Lens cleaning blower and microfiber cloth

---

## Step 1: Prepare and Deburr 3D Parts
1. Clean all cured resin / filament strings from the sliding channel slots.
2. Slide the empty carriage into the rail mount from the side.
3. Verify that the carriage slides smoothly from end to end across the full **28.0 mm stroke**. If there is any friction, follow the wet-sanding steps in [PRINTING_GUIDE.md](PRINTING_GUIDE.md).

---

## Step 2: Install Magnetic Detents
The magnets provide a satisfying, positive tactile "snap" when the lens locks into the **Parked (0 mm)** or **Active (28 mm)** position.

1. Locate the small cylindrical magnet recesses on the carriage and the rail track.
2. **Check Polarities:** Before applying glue, ensure the magnets attract each other at both stops rather than repel.
3. Place a tiny droplet of B-7000 or superglue into each pocket using the tip of a toothpick.
4. Press the magnets flush into the pockets. Let cure for at least 30 minutes before sliding.

---

## Step 3: Apply Scratch-Prevention Microfiber Pads
To guarantee zero risk of micro-scratches from trapped pocket grit:
1. Cut two small strips (approx. 2 mm × 15 mm) of 0.3 mm adhesive-backed microfiber tape.
2. Stick them to the underside contact feet of the sliding carriage (the surfaces facing the phone's camera glass).
3. Confirm that the carriage maintains a ~0.4–0.5 mm raised air gap above the camera glass window.

---

## Step 4: Install the Optical Lens Core
1. Blow away all dust from the Pixel 8 camera visor and both sides of your optical lens core using a manual lens blower.
2. Align the optical element with the Ø 15.6 mm bore in the lens carriage.
3. Gently press-fit the lens barrel until it seats against the internal retaining collar.
4. If the fit is slightly loose, apply a microscopic smear of B-7000 around the outer rim of the lens barrel (avoiding glass surfaces).

---

## Step 5: Mount Rail to the Pixel 8 Visor
1. Clean the perimeter of the Pixel 8 camera visor with an alcohol prep pad.
2. Align the top and bottom hooks of the **Visor Rail Mount** with the upper and lower ridges of the camera visor.
3. Snap the mount firmly into place: the upper lip hooks over the top aluminum bevel, and the lower lip snaps under the bottom ridge.
4. Slide the lens carriage into the rail channel.

---

## Step 6: Alignment & Optical Verification
1. Open the **Google Camera (GCam)** app on your Pixel 8.
2. Switch to the standard **1x Main Camera** mode.
3. Slide the carriage to the **Active (28 mm)** position until it clicks.
4. Check the viewfinder:
   * **Centering:** Image should be bright with uniform edge illumination (no off-axis vignette).
   * **Focus:** Tap to focus on a nearby subject (~1–2 meters). The camera's laser autofocus will focus sharply through the auxiliary telephoto element.
   * **Magnification:** You will observe a true 2x–2.5x optical field-of-view shift with natural background blur and zero computational crop noise.
5. Slide the carriage back to **Parked (0 mm)**:
   * Viewfinder should return to the clean, unobstructed 1x wide view.
   * Switch to **0.5x Ultrawide** — verify that the parked carriage does not clip the ultra-wide field of view.

Enjoy your new hot-swappable telephoto camera! 📸
